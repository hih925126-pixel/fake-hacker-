ABOUT & SAFETY

        

          
NEBULA is a fictional entertainment simulator. Nothing in it touches real systems. All targets, devices, addresses, alerts, logs and traffic are generated locally in your browser and use reserved documentation address ranges and .invalid domains.

          
It never scans networks, reads your Wi-Fi or router, captures passwords, intercepts traffic, performs real decryption, contacts external servers, installs software or changes system settings. The only data stored is your progress and preferences, kept in this browser's local storage.

          
Shortcuts: Alt+1…8 switch views · ↑↓ command history · Tab complete · Ctrl+C abort · Ctrl+L clear
